fix(vescuart): actually claim motor_uart when switching drivers

The VescUART driver could stay silent forever after a driver switch, with
every value reading zero.

Run() retried the port claim with:

    if (!uartport->isReserved()) {
        acquirePort();
    }

but UARTPort::isReserved() is also true when somebody ELSE holds the port.
Axis::setDrvType() constructs the new driver before destroying the old one,
so switching from VescCAN (7) to VescUART (13) meant reservePort() failed at
construction time and the retry condition never became true again. The driver
never configured USART3, never armed the receive interrupt and never sent a
byte - which is why crcerrors/uarterrors (inbound only) could not tell the
difference between "no VESC" and "driver mute".

Add UARTPort::isOwnedBy() and retry while we are not the owner. Also use it
for the forceposread gate.

Add the diagnostic counters that found this, since the existing ones cannot
distinguish an idle link from a dead one:

  * txcount    - packets put on the wire, and transmits the HAL refused
  * rxcount    - raw bytes seen by the USART

Verified on hardware after flashing: vescstate=4, fwversion=1538 (6.02),
hwname=MKSESC_75_100_V2, encrate=333, crcerrors=0, uarterrors=0,
errorflags=0, pos=18750 (about 1.875 turns). VescCAN on drvtype 7 still
works while the UART port is claimed, so the two coexist.
